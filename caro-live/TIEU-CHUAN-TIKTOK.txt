TIEU CHUAN LIVE CUA TIKTOK VA CACH GAME NAY TRANH VI PHAM  (cap nhat ban 45)
(Tra cuu 28/11/2026. Phan Community Guidelines lay tu trang chinh thuc cua TikTok. Phan LIVE Monetization / gift / FYF: trang chinh thuc
 khong doc duoc tu dong, nen lay tu cac ban tom tat thu hai -> hay doi chieu lai trong ung dung:
 TikTok > Cai dat > LIVE Safety / Trung tam an toan LIVE. TikTok hay doi luat.)

1) TIKTOK QUY DINH GI (tom tat)
- Ai LIVE va ai tang qua deu phai tu 18 tuoi. Vi pham: bi dong phien LIVE, han che LIVE hoac khoa tai khoan.
- Dua nguoi xem ra ngoai TikTok (link, ma QR, Zalo, Facebook, so dien thoai, mua hang...) bi cam.
- "Gift baiting": xin qua may moc / lap di lap lai, hua thuong hoac dieu kien (tang qua moi duoc choi, tang qua de duoc chon), gay ap luc cam xuc,
  dat 2 phe doi dau nhau bang qua / tim / binh luan.
- Co bac, bốc tham, tra tien de co co hoi thang giai: cam. Quang cao nha cai / ca do: cam.
- Noi dung khong phai cua ban (video dang lai, co logo / watermark cua nguoi khac), phim hoat hinh / nhac khong ban quyen: khong duoc de xuat, co the bi go
  (nhac duoc kiem tra ban quyen theo thoi gian thuc).
- LIVE phat lap, khong nguoi, hoac gan nhu hoan toan do tu dong / AI dieu khien: bi coi la chat luong thap, co the khong duoc kiem tien / de xuat.
- Chu phong chiu trach nhiem moi thu phat trong LIVE: chu hien, tieng doc, nhac, video, thong bao, cong cu ben thu ba doc binh luan.

2) GAME DA LAM GI DE TRANH (ban 45)
- Da go MOI cau KEU GOI tang qua: man hinh cho, cau MC moi vao, huong dan, nhac nho, thong bao. Chi con cau CAM ON sau khi co nguoi tang qua.
- Man hinh khong con hien "(+N diem)" canh ten mon qua.
- Cong tac giftPoints (file config.js): true = qua van cong diem tuong tac de duoc chon len ban (nhu cu); false = qua CHI de cam on, KHONG anh huong ai duoc chon.
  Rui ro con lai neu de true: TikTok co the xem "qua -> co hoi duoc choi" la dieu kien hoa noi dung bang qua. AN TOAN NHAT: dat giftPoints: false.
- Tim / follow / share van cong diem; tim chi de xac nhan "san sang", khong ep, khong hua thuong.
- Nhac nho follow / share thua hon (300 giay), viet nhe, khong xin xo.
- Video an mung MAC DINH TAT. Cac video trong public\media la cua nguoi khac (co logo TikTok / hoat hinh), de bi danh dau ban quyen / watermark.
  Chi bat lai (o bang dieu khien, muc video an mung) khi da thay bang video CUA BAN hoac mien phi ban quyen.
- Bo loc ten + binh luan hien tren man hinh (src\safety.js): che link, so dien thoai, tu tho tuc, quang cao nha cai / co bac / tai xiu / no hu,
  kiem tien / lua dao, noi dung nguoi lon, ma tuy; ke ca khi viet tach chu (n.h.a c.a.i) hoac khong dau.
- Thong bao va cau thoai MC tu sua trong bang dieu khien: game TU CHOI neu co link, so dien thoai, Zalo..., keu goi tang qua, co bac, tu tho tuc.
  Cau thoai cu da luu ma vi pham se tu bi bo qua khi mo game.
- Bo toan bo yeu to bao luc trong cau thoai / bieu tuong. Khong co giai thuong, khong boc tham.
- Che do DEMO luon hien dai chu do "CHE DO THU" tren man hinh live, de khong lo phat nham len LIVE that.

3) CHU PHONG NEN LAM
- Phai co mat va noi chuyen that. Dung treo LIVE tu dong 24/7. Game chi nen la 1 phan cua buoi LIVE; doc binh luan, tro chuyen bang giong cua ban.
- Giong MC la giong may (TTS). Neu TikTok co muc gan nhan "noi dung do AI tao ra / giong tong hop", hay bat.
- KHONG bat DEMO khi LIVE that. Dung ep, dung nan ni tang qua; dung hua tang tien / qua / the cao cho nguoi thang.
- Nhac: chi dung nhac khong ban quyen (thu muc public\music). Khong bo file thi game tu tao nhac nen nhe, an toan.
- Neu thay ten xau bo qua loc: chan / bao cao nguoi do trong TikTok.
- Chay thu 10-15 phut truoc, vao LIVE Safety Center xem co thong bao vi pham nao khong.

4) NHUNG DIEU CHUA THE DAM BAO (noi that)
- Khong the cam ket TikTok se khong nhac nho / han che; luat doi lien tuc va nhieu muc lay tu ban tom tat thu hai.
- Game doc binh luan, tim, qua qua thu vien khong chinh thuc (tiktok-live-connector) hoac TikFinity. TikTok khong phat hanh hai cong cu nay;
  neu thay canh bao khi dung, hay ngung va xem lai.
- Game chua duoc thu tren LIVE that qua nhieu gio; hay theo doi 1-2 buoi dau.
