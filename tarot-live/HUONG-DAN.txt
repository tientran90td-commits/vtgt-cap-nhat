TAROT LIVE v1.0 - HUONG DAN NHANH (Vietnamese, khong dau de tranh loi font)
====================================================================
Game boi bai Tarot cho TikTok LIVE: khan gia binh luan de rut bai, la bai lat len man hinh (co anh), MC doc y nghia.
Chi mang tinh GIAI TRI va goi mo. Game KHONG du doan suc khoe, tien bac, sinh tu, phap ly.

1) CAI DAT (chi lam 1 lan)
   - Bam dup  CAI-MOI-TRUONG.bat  -> tu tai va cai Node.js + thu vien (can Internet). Cac file CHAY-*.bat cung tu goi file nay.
   - Giai nen thu muc game ra o dia, vi du D:\tarot-live.

2) CHAY THU (khong can LIVE that)
   - Bam dup  CHAY-THU-DEMO.bat -> Edge mo bang dieu khien (http://localhost:3002). Khan gia ao tu binh luan xin bai.
   - Man hinh live luon hien dai do "CHE DO THU". KHONG phat che do nay len LIVE that.
   - Game dung cong 3002 (Caro la 3000) nen chay chung 2 game cung luc duoc.

3) CHAY THAT
   - Bam dup  CHAY-GAME.bat, nhap ID TikTok (khong co @). Tai khoan phai DANG LIVE.
   - Hoac dung TikFinity Desktop: mo TikFinity, ket noi, roi bam dup  CHAY-TIKFINITY.bat.

4) CACH KHAN GIA CHOI (go vao binh luan)
   - !bai            rut 1 la (so la mac dinh sua duoc trong Cai dat)
   - !bai 3          rut 3 la (Qua khu - Hien tai - Tuong lai gan)
   - !bai 5          rut 5 la (Tinh huong - Nguyen nhan - Tro ngai - Loi khuyen - Ket qua co the), moi luot khoang 1,5 phut
   - !bai tinh yeu / !bai cong viec (hoac hoc tap)   chon chu de. Vi du:  !bai 3 tinh yeu   |   boi bai cong viec 5 la
   - Cung hieu: "rut bai", "xem bai", "boi bai", "xem tarot", "!tarot".
   - Hoi ve suc khoe / tien bac / kien tung / co bac...: game van rut nhung chi doc thong diep chung va noi ro la khong xem muc do.
   - Moi nguoi 1 luot trong hang cho; xong phai cho (mac dinh 90 giay) moi xin lai (sua trong Cai dat).

5) BANG DIEU KHIEN (http://localhost:3002)
   - "MO MAN HINH LIVE DOC": mo 1 tab sach (9:16). Dung tab do lam nguon hinh cho OBS / TikTok LIVE Studio.
     OBS Browser Source:  http://localhost:3002/live.html?layout=portrait   (ngang: layout=landscape)
   - Giong doc phat ra tu TAB MAN HINH LIVE; bam chuot vao tab do 1 lan. Dung Microsoft Edge de co giong tieng Viet tu nhien.
     Phim tat trong tab live: F toan man hinh, M tat/bat giong, P doc, L ngang.
   - Hang cho: nut "Uu tien" day nguoi len dau, "Xoa" bo nguoi do. "Bo qua luot nay", "Tam ngung nhan luot moi", "Xoa hang cho".
   - "Tu rut cho mot nguoi": ban tu nhap ten va rut ngay. "Chay thu 1/3/5 la" de xem truoc.
   - Cai dat tu luu: ten chuong trinh, dong phu, so la mac dinh, cho phep 3 la / 5 la, ti le la nguoc, thoi gian cho, tu rut khi vang nguoi, giong doc.
   - Thong bao chu phong: co the lap lai moi N phut; game tu choi neu co link, so dien thoai, Zalo/Facebook, moi mua hang...
   - Muon thu nhanh khong can khan gia: mo trinh duyet vao  http://localhost:3002/test?u=An&c=!bai%203

6) BO BAI VA LOI DOC
   - Anh: bo Rider-Waite-Smith (1909) cua Pamela Colman Smith, da het ban quyen (public domain). Xem public\cards\NGUON-ANH.txt.
   - Loi doc 78 la (xuoi / nguoc, tong quat / tinh duyen / cong viec) va loi khuyen rieng cho tung la do tac gia game tu viet bang tieng Viet.
   - Trai 3 / 5 la: luon UP ca bai roi LAT HET 1 LUOT, sau do doc tung la.
   - Moi vi tri trong trai bai co cach doc rieng (vi du "Tro ngai" doc mat can luu y cua la, "Loi khuyen" doc loi khuyen rieng).
   - Muon sua chu: mo src\cards\*.js (major, wands, cups, swords, pents) va src\cards\advice.js bang Notepad, giu nguyen dau nhay va dau phay.

7) LUU Y
   - Muon phat len TikTok tu may tinh can phan mem LIVE (OBS / TikTok LIVE Studio).
   - Mat ket noi TikTok / TikFinity thi game tu noi lai.
   - Cap nhat: bang dieu khien -> "Kiem tra ban moi" -> "Cap nhat ngay" (giu nguyen cai dat va ID TikTok).
   - Xem them TIEU-CHUAN-TIKTOK.txt.

7) HOI THONG TIN NGUOI DUOC CHON (mac dinh BAT)
   - Den luot ai, man hinh hien khung dem nguoc (60 giay): nguoi do binh luan  ho ten, ngay/thang/nam sinh, chuyen muon xem.
     Vi du:  Lan, 20/08/1998, tinh duyen   hoac   Ten Minh sinh ngay 3 thang 8 nam 1990 muon xem cong viec
     Co the chia lam nhieu binh luan. Du ngay sinh + (ten hoac chuyen muon xem) thi game bat dau sau 3 giay. Het gio ma chua gui thi doc thong diep chung.
   - Game tinh CUNG HOANG DAO va SO CHU DAO tu ngay sinh, MC noi trong loi chao. Man hinh KHONG hien ngay sinh, game KHONG luu ngay sinh / cau hoi ra file.
   - Tat o Cai dat: bo dau "Nguoi duoc chon phai binh luan...". Chu phong tu rut cung co o nhap ngay sinh / chuyen muon xem.
   - Cau hoi ve suc khoe, tien bac, kien tung, co bac...: van doc nhung chi thong diep chung.

8) AI VIET LOI DOC (khong bat buoc, mac dinh TAT)
   - Dung de moi lan rut deu co loi doc rieng theo ten, cung, chuyen muon xem va dung cac la da rut. Trai 5 la doc khoang 2 phut.
   - Cach bat: tao file  ai-api-key.txt  (cung thu muc voi CHAY-GAME.bat), chi dan 1 dong la khoa API (khong them chu nao khac).
     Roi vao bang dieu khien > Cai dat > "AI viet loi doc" chon Claude hoac Gemini. De trong "Ten mau AI" de dung mau mac dinh.
   - Game gui cho AI: ten, cung hoang dao, so chu dao, chu de, chuyen muon xem (da loc), ten + vi tri + xuoi/nguoc cua cac la. KHONG gui ngay sinh day du.
     Xem mau form o file  MAU-FORM-AI.txt.
   - AI loi / cham qua 9 giay / tra ve noi dung la (link, so dien thoai, tu cam) -> game tu doc bang loi viet san, khong bi dung.
   - Chi phi: tinh theo luot, bang gia cua nha cung cap. Khoa bi lo thi doi khoa ngay. File ai-api-key.txt khong nam trong file zip / kho GitHub.
   - Goi Gemini mien phi co the bi Google dung du lieu de cai thien san pham: nen biet truoc khi dung.
