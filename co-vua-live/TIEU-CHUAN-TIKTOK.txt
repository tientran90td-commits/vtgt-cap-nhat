TIEU CHUAN LIVE CUA TIKTOK VA CACH GAME NAY TRANH VI PHAM
(Tra cuu ngay 25/11/2026 tu trang Community Guidelines cua TikTok (muc Accounts and Features, Integrity and Authenticity),
 thong bao LIVE Monetization Guidelines va chinh sach gift. TikTok hay doi luat, hay xem lai trong ung dung:
 TikTok > Cai dat > LIVE Safety / Trung tam an toan LIVE.)

1) TIKTOK QUY DINH GI (tom tat)
- Ai LIVE va ai tang qua deu phai tu 18 tuoi. Noi dung vi pham se bi DONG phien LIVE, co the bi han che LIVE hoac khoa tai khoan.
- LIVE co muc dich chinh la dua nguoi xem ra ngoai TikTok (link, Zalo, Facebook, so dien thoai, mua hang...) bi cam / khong duoc de xuat.
- Noi dung "lua hoac thao tung de tang qua, follow, tim, view" hoac hua thuong gia de doi tuong tac -> khong duoc de xuat len muc Danh cho ban (FYF),
  co the bi han che kiem tien.
- Noi dung gia tao, chat luong thap, lap lai khong duoc kiem tien tu LIVE (LIVE Monetization Guidelines).
- Co bac / bốc tham / tra tien de co co hoi thang giai thuong bi cam (chinh sach TikTok Shop dinh nghia ro: dua tien hoac vat gia tri de co co hoi thang giai).
- Chu phong chiu trach nhiem moi thu phat trong LIVE, ke ca nhac, video, thong bao.

2) GAME DA LAM GI DE TRANH
- Da bo toan bo cau thoai, thong bao, bieu tuong co yeu to bao luc (set danh, bao thu, ⚔, 💥...).
- Nhac follow / chia se khong con noi "duoc cong diem". Dong tin tuc khi co nguoi follow / share chi cam on, khong khoe diem thuong.
- Ten nguoi xem (nickname TikTok) hien tren man hinh va duoc MC doc to: tu dong BO link va che tu tho tuc, thay bang "Khan gia 123" (file src\safety.js).
- Thong bao cua chu phong: game TU CHOI neu co link, so dien thoai, Zalo/Facebook/Telegram, moi mua hang / kiem tien / dau tu, hoac tu tho tuc.
- Che do DEMO (khan gia ao): luon hien dai chu do "CHE DO THU" tren man hinh live, de khong lo phat nham len LIVE that
  (tuong tac gia la vi pham). Bang dieu khien cung ghi ro.
- Game KHONG co giai thuong, khong boc tham, khong ban co hoi thang bang tien/qua. Qua tang chi lam tang diem tuong tac de duoc chon len ban.
  Dung giu nguyen nhu vay: DUNG hua tang tien / qua / the cao cho nguoi thang, va dung hua "tang qua de chac chan duoc choi".

3) CHU PHONG NEN LAM
- KHONG bat che do DEMO khi dang LIVE that. KHONG dung o "Go thu" de gia lam khan gia khi LIVE that.
- Phai co nguoi that dang ngoi LIVE; dung treo LIVE tu dong 24/7 khong co nguoi (de bi coi la noi dung tu dong / chat luong thap).
- Dung ep, dung nan nỉ tang qua; dung noi cau kieu "tang qua moi duoc choi". Cau moi trong game da duoc viet nhe nhang.
- Nhac: chi dung nhac khong ban quyen (thu muc public\music). Neu khong bo file, game tu tao nhac nen nhe, an toan.
- Video an mung (public\media\celebrate.mp4 / .webm): chi giu neu hinh VA tieng la cua ban hoac mien phi ban quyen. Neu khong chac, hay thay bang video cua ban.
- Avatar, ten nguoi xem hien tren man hinh: neu thay ten xau bo qua loc, vao TikTok chan/bao cao nguoi do.
- Chay thu 10-15 phut truoc, vao LIVE Safety Center xem co thong bao vi pham nao khong.

4) NHUNG DIEU CHUA THE DAM BAO (noi that)
- Khong the cam ket TikTok se khong nhac nho / han che. Cac cau thoai tu sua cua ban (data\thoai.json) cung nam trong trach nhiem cua ban.
- Game doc binh luan, tim, qua qua thu vien khong chinh thuc (tiktok-live-connector) hoac TikFinity. TikTok khong phat hanh hai cong cu nay;
  neu thay TikTok canh bao khi dung, hay ngung va xem lai.
- Game chua duoc thu tren LIVE that qua nhieu gio; hay theo doi 1-2 buoi dau.
