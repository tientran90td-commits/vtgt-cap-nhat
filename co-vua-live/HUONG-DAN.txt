     Cac file CHAY-*.bat cung tu goi file nay neu may chua co Node.js.
   - Cach thu cong (neu cach nhanh loi):
   - Cai Node.js ban LTS (>= 20) tu https://nodejs.org  (cu bam Next den het).
   - Giai nen thu muc game ra o dia, vi du D:\co-vua-live.

2) CHAY THU (khong can LIVE that)
   - Bam dup file  CHAY-THU-DEMO.bat
   - Trinh duyet Edge tu mo bang dieu khien. Khan gia ao se tu binh luan, tha tim, tang qua.

3) CHAY THAT

   - Ban co the gioi han toc do tot hon bang cach dan khoa Euler vao file euler-api-key.txt (khong bat buoc).

4) BANG DIEU KHIEN (http://localhost:3000)
   - Nut "MO MAN HINH LIVE DOC" se mo 1 TAB MOI sach se (9:16). Dung tab do lam nguon hinh.
     + OBS: Them nguon "Window Capture"/"Browser Source" voi dia chi http://localhost:3000/live.html?layout=portrait
     + TikTok LIVE Studio: them nguon "Window/Screen capture" chon cua so tab do.
   - Giong doc phat ra tu TAB MAN HINH LIVE. Hay bam vao tab do 1 lan (hoac mo bang file .bat, da co san co cho phep).
     De thu tren may tinh/OBS, thu am thanh may (Desktop Audio) de khan gia nghe thay.
   - Dung Microsoft Edge de co giong doc tieng Viet tu nhien (HoaiMy, NamMinh).
   - "Chay DEMO": bat/tat khan gia ao ngay tren bang dieu khien.
   - "Sua thoai": sua cau noi cua Dieu phoi vien va Trong tai. Xuong dong = nhieu cach noi (chon ngau nhien).
     Bam o xanh {ten}, {qua}... de chen ten/qua. Co muc "Cam on khi nhan qua", "Cam on follow", "Cam on chia se".
   - "Go thu": gia lam khan gia/nguoi choi de thu binh luan, tha tim, tang qua.

5) CACH DOC TEN SAI
   - Mo data\cach-doc-ten.json, them dong:  "ten that": "cach doc dung"
     vi du  "vnpsolar": "ve en pe so la"

6) LUAT VA CACH GO NUOC DI
   - Co vua chuan: nhap thanh, bat tot qua duong, phong cap, chieu, chieu het. Quan TRANG di truoc (tung dong xu chon ai cam quan trang).
     Hoa khi: het nuoc di (pat), khong du quan, lap lai the co 3 lan, 50 nuoc khong an quan / di tot.
   - Nguoi choi den luot go nuoc di trong binh luan. Game hieu nhieu cach go:
       e4  E4  @@e4  |  e2e4  e2 e4  e2-e4  |  Nf3  Nxe5  exd5  O-O  O-O-O  e8=Q
       ma f3  xe d1  tuong c4  hau h5  tot e4  vua g1  |  ma g1 f3 (quan + o di + o den)
       nhap thanh (ngan / dai)  |  e7 e8 hau (phong cap; khong noi thi len HAU)  |  'minh di e4 nhe'
   - Go ngan nhat: chi go O DEN, vi du  e4  -> game tu biet quan nao di toi do.
     Neu co nhieu quan cung di duoc (vi du f3: tot f2 hoac ma g1) man hinh nhac:  go  tot f3  hoac  ma f3 .
   - Go 2 lan cung duoc: go  e2  (chon quan: o do sang len, cac o di duoc hien cham xanh, o an quan hien vong do) roi go  e4 .
     Chon quan co hieu luc 20 giay.
   - Ghi ky hieu (a1, e4...) mo mo ngay trong tung o co de nguoi xem biet go gi. Tat/bat: Bang dieu khien -> Cai dat -> 'Ghi ky hieu trong o co'.
   - Chu phong go  !reset  de lam moi. Nguoi choi go  !dauhang  de dau hang.

7) LUU Y

   - Neu loi ket noi: kiem tra ID, tai khoan dang LIVE, mang.

   - Sau nay chuyen sang PC: giai nen cung thu muc, chay CHAY-GAME.bat.

9) NHAN VAT 3D GIONG NGUOI THAT (tuy chon)
   - Game da san sang nap model .glb co khung xuong. Dat file vao thu muc  public\models\
       nam.glb = nhan vat nam (cam quan TRANG)     nu.glb = nhan vat nu (cam quan DEN)
     Khong co file -> game dung nhan vat dung san.
   - Cach tao model tu 2 anh tham chieu: dung trang web "anh -> 3D" co tu dong gan xuong (vi du Tripo, Meshy):
       1. Tai anh nhan vat len, tao model 3D, bat "auto rig" (gan xuong nguoi), tai ve dang .glb
       2. Neu trang khong gan xuong: tai len mixamo.com de tu dong gan xuong, tai ve roi doi sang .glb
   - Thu nhanh: mo  http://localhost:3000/live.html?nam=models/ten-file.glb  de thu 1 model.
   - Dang cai tren man hinh:  ?model=0  tat model that, dung nhan vat dung san.

10) HIEU UNG, NHAC FOLLOW, CHINH GIONG (moi)
   - Tieng di quan, bang chu CHIEU / AN QUAN / CHIEU HET (co chu noi bat), phao hoa + nhac khi co nguoi thang, tieng dem nguoc, chuong cam on qua.
     Bat/tat + chinh am luong trong the "Am thanh & hieu ung" o bang dieu khien. Co nut nghe thu.
   - Nhac follow / chia se: cu moi 150 giay (doi trong "Cai dat van dau", ghi 0 de tat) hien khung tren man hinh + dieu phoi doc.
     Sua cau nhac o muc "Sua thoai" -> nhom "Nhac nho". Follow/chia se duoc cong diem tuong tac (cai trong config.js muc points).
   - Chinh giong: the "Giong doc" co thanh toc do / cao do cho Dieu phoi va Trong tai, kem nut mau (Tuoi vui, Diu dang, Nang dong / Tram nghiem, Ro rang, Hao hung).
     Giong tu nhien nhat: Microsoft Edge tren Windows (HoaiMy, NamMinh).

11) ANH DAI DIEN + NHAC NEN (moi)
   - Anh dai dien TikTok cua nguoi choi hien trong bang ten tren dau nhan vat 3D (vien vang cho quan Trang, xanh cho quan Den) va o the nguoi choi.
     Che do demo dung anh chu cai tu tao. Neu TikTok khong tra anh thi hien huy hieu T (Trang) / D (Den).
   - Nhac nen: bat/tat + chinh am luong trong the "Am thanh & hieu ung". Tu ha nho khi dieu phoi vien / trong tai dang noi.
     Mac dinh game tu tao nhac nhe. Muon dung nhac rieng: bo file mp3/ogg/wav/m4a vao thu muc  public\music  (nen dung nhac khong ban quyen,
     vi TikTok co the tat tieng hoac chan live neu dung nhac co ban quyen).

12) DUNG TIKFINITY LAM NGUON SU KIEN (tuy chon, chi tren may tinh Windows)
   - TikFinity Desktop (tikfinity.zerody.one) tu ket noi TikTok LIVE roi phat su kien qua cong 21213 tren chinh may do.
   - Cach dung: mo TikFinity Desktop -> ket noi tai khoan dang LIVE -> bam dup  CHAY-TIKFINITY.bat
     (khong can nhap ID TikTok, khong can API key). Bang dieu khien se hien "Da noi TikFinity".
   - QUAN TRONG: phai dung ung dung TikFinity DESKTOP (tai ve cai tren Windows), KHONG phai ban web tikfinity.zerody.one mo bang trinh duyet.
     Chi ban Desktop moi mo cong 21213 cho game doc. Ban web co dong chu 'Phim tat ban phim khong co san trong trinh duyet'.
     Dang dung ban web thi dong tab di (tranh bao 'Duplicate execution'), mo ung dung Desktop, dang nhap va bam TRUC TIEP.
   - Cua so den cua game se ghi 'TikFinity gui su kien ...' khi co binh luan / tim / qua; o trang thai tren bang dieu khien
     hien 'da nhan N su kien'. Neu N khong tang khi ban tang qua thi TikFinity chua gui gi cho game.
   - Neu khong noi duoc: kiem tra TikFinity dang chay cung may va cong la 21213 (neu khac, sua tikfinityUrl trong config.js).
   - Cach cu (CHAY-GAME.bat) van dung duoc; chon cach nao on dinh hon voi ban.

13) THA TIM XAC NHAN + NHAC KHI BINH LUAN BI AN
   - Sau khi chon duoc nguoi choi, 2 nguoi do phai THA TIM trong 20 giay de xac nhan se choi (man hinh hien dem nguoc).
     Ai khong tha tim se mat suat (bi tru het diem) va nguoi xep ke tiep duoc moi thay. Het nguoi thay thi chon lai tu dau.
     Nguoi dang giu ban (thang lien tiep) khong can tha tim lai. Chu phong bam nut 'Bo qua xac nhan tim' de vao van ngay.
     Doi so giay (0 = tat) trong Bang dieu khien -> Cai dat -> 'Giay de 2 nguoi duoc chon THA TIM xac nhan'.
   - BIEN BAO NHO o goc man hinh live (KHONG doc thanh tieng) ghi san: cach go nuoc di, 'Neu binh luan khong hien len, them 1 hoac 2 dau @
     o truoc nuoc di' (TikTok hay an binh luan bi nghi spam), va go !them de xin them gio. Khi vao luot ma nguoi choi im lang qua 12 giay,
     bien bao se nhap nhay de goi chu y. Game doc duoc ca  @e4  va  @@e4  nhu nuoc di binh thuong.
     Chinh trong Bang dieu khien -> Cai dat: 'Nguoi choi im lang sau ... giay thi bien bao nhap nhay' (0 = tat), tick/bo tick 'Hien bien bao nho'.
   - TIENG TICH TAC: khi nguoi choi con 15 giay, dong ho keu tich-tac (5 giay cuoi to hon). Doi so giay (0 = tat) o muc 'Tieng tich tac khi con ... giay'.
   - Cac cau thoai moi nam trong Bang dieu khien -> Loi thoai (nhom 'Nhac nho' va 'Xac nhan tha tim'), sua duoc.

14) TU SANG VAN KE TIEP + TU KHOI DONG LAI
   - Het van, game tu hien ket qua roi tu sang van moi: nguoi thang o lai, cac ban tich diem trong luc xem duoc xep hang san,
     nen vong cho chon nguoi choi chi con 10 giay (Bang dieu khien -> Cai dat -> 'Giay cho ... (cac van sau)'). Bo tick
     'Het van tu sang van ke tiep' neu muon cho lau nhu van dau. Khong can bam gi them de choi tiep.
   - Neu game bi loi dung dot ngot, file CHAY-*.bat tu khoi dong lai sau 5 giay (toi da 10 lan lien tiep).
   - Mat ket noi TikTok / TikFinity thi game tu noi lai, khong can tat mo.

15) GIONG DOC: CHUA VAO TRAN NOI NHIEU, TRONG TRAN NOI IT NHAT
   - Luc CHUA VAO TRAN (cho nguoi choi, xac nhan tha tim, tung xu, het van): MC chao nguoi moi vao (khi ho binh luan lan dau),
     giai thich cach choi o dau moi vong cho, cam on nguoi tang qua / follow / chia se.
   - Luc DANG DI CO: gan nhu im lang, chi doc ket qua chieu het / hoa. Qua tang trong tran duoc don lai, cam on ngay sau tran.
   - Bang dieu khien -> Giong doc -> 'Muc do noi':
       It   : gan nhu im lang ca luc cho (chi ket qua tung xu, thang/thua, cam on qua LON sau tran).
       Vua  (mac dinh): nhu mo ta o tren.
       Nhieu: them doc tung nuoc di ("ma an f ba, chieu"), canh bao het gio trong tran.
   - Cac cau van hien chu tren man hinh (khung thong bao) du khong doc thanh tieng.
   - Sua loi chao / giai thich: Bang dieu khien -> Loi thoai (nhom 'Mo dau': 'Giai thich cach choi', 'Chao nguoi moi vao').

18) TU CHON 2 NGUOI XEM CHO DAU VOI NHAU (SOLO)

   - Bam vao ten de chon dung 2 nguoi (so 1, 2 hien ben trai), roi bam 'Cho 2 nguoi dau'. Game tung dong xu chon ben di truoc va vao van ngay.
     Muon doi nguoi: bam lai vao ten da chon de bo chon, hoac 'Bo chon'.
   - Mac dinh KHONG bat tha tim xac nhan (vi ban da chon). Tick 'Bat 2 nguoi tha tim xac nhan...' neu muon ho phai tha tim truoc.
   - Dang co van ma bam 'Cho 2 nguoi dau' thi game hoi lai, dong y thi van cu bi dung. Sau van solo, game tro lai tu chon nhu binh thuong.

19) BANG TOP TUONG TAC O GOC PHAI MAN HINH + GO !diem XEM HANG
   - Diem tinh tu luc van truoc BAT DAU (luc tung dong xu, diem cua moi nguoi duoc xoa ve 0). Trong luc dang choi, khan gia
     binh luan / tha tim / tang qua / follow / chia se de tich diem. Het van, 2 nguoi diem cao nhat (top 1 va top 2) duoc chon dau van ke tiep
     (neu nguoi thang o lai thi chi chon 1 nguoi). Bat dau van moi thi diem lai ve 0.
   - Bang 'TOP TUONG TAC' nam o GOC PHAI man hinh live (de khong bi o binh luan cua TikTok o ben trai che), hien 6 nguoi dau, co ten + diem; 2 o dau duoc to vang (nguoi se duoc chon). Nguoi dang dau co dau kiem.
   - Khan gia go  !diem  (hoac !top, !hang) trong binh luan: man hinh hien hang + diem cua ho, va con thieu bao nhieu diem de vao top 2.
     Cau nay chi hien chu, khong doc thanh tieng.
   - Cach tinh diem (binh luan, tim, qua, follow, chia se) sua trong config.js muc points.
     Thu tu diem: QUA > FOLLOW (100) > CHIA SE (60) > THA TIM (1 diem/tim). Mac dinh KHONG gioi han diem (likeCapPerRound, giftCapPerRound, likeMaxPerEvent = 0).
     Qua cang dat cang nhieu diem (Hoa hong ~175, qua 30 kim cuong ~340, qua 1000 kim cuong ~1700), nhung tang cham dan
     de nguoi tang qua nho khong ap dao han. Muon gioi han thi dat so lon hon 0 cho cac muc cap.
     Muon nghieng ve ben nao thi doi giftBase / giftPerDiamond / likeCapPerRound trong config.js.

CAP NHAT TRUC TIEP (khong can tai lai ban zip)
   - Tren bang dieu khien co the "Cap nhat phan mem": bam "Kiem tra ban moi", neu co thi bam "Cap nhat ngay".
   - Tool tu tai ban moi tu GitHub, thay file, roi tu mo lai (khoang 10 giay). Can mo game bang CHAY-GAME.bat (hoac chay-termux.sh) de no tu mo lai.
   - Giu nguyen: cai dat, thong ke (thu muc data), ID TikTok (ten-tiktok.txt). File bi thay se duoc sao luu o data/backup-ban-<so>/
     (neu ban tu sua config.js, ban cu nam trong thu muc sao luu do).
   - Dang LIVE thi nen cap nhat luc nghi giua ca vi game se tat/mo lai.

CHONG AUTO-CLICK THA TIM
- Nguoi bam tay thi bam tung dot roi nghi, app auto-click thi bam deu deu khong nghi. Moi nguoi co "binh suc bam tim":
  het suc thi luot tim chi duoc tinh 1 phan nho (khong ve 0). Nghi 1 chut la suc hoi lai.
- Bang dieu khien > cai dat van > "Chong auto-click tha tim": Vua (khuyen dung) / Long / Chat / Tat.
- Nguoi nghi auto-click hien dau canh bao trong bang xep hang cua bang dieu khien va co dong thong bao.
- Chi anh huong diem tha tim. Qua tang, follow, chia se va viec "tha tim xac nhan len dai" khong bi anh huong.
- Muon chinh so: sua file src\likeguard.js (muc PRESETS).

VIDEO AN MUNG KHI CO NGUOI THANG
- Co nguoi thang van -> nhac cua video phat ngay, 4 giay sau hinh moi hien len thay cho 2 nhan vat o duoi. Neu trinh duyet khong phat duoc mp4 thi tu dung ban webm (celebrate.webm). Het video thi tro ve nhu cu
  (2 nhan vat quay lai) va game chay cac buoc tiep theo binh thuong. Man hinh ket qua duoc giu it nhat 22 giay de video chay het.
- Bat/tat: Bang dieu khien > cai dat van > "Video an mung khi co nguoi thang".
- Doi video khac: thay file public\media\celebrate.mp4 (nen dung mp4 ma H.264 + AAC, hinh vuong cho dep). Neu video dai hon, tang
  celebrateMinSeconds trong config.js.
- Trinh duyet co the tat tieng video cho den khi bam vao trang 1 lan. Trong OBS / TikFinity thuong van co tieng.
- MC khen nguoi thang "ba dao, ba khi...": sua o muc Loi thoai (winGame, congrats, winForfeit, streak). Neu ban da tung sua loi thoai
  (data\thoai.json), bam "Khoi phuc tat ca thoai goc" de nhan cau khen moi.
- Thang vao ngay len cap: co dong thong bao, banner "DOT PHA" va MC chuc mung (sua cau o muc Loi thoai > rankUp).
- Rank lay tu so van thang da co san: nguoi da thang nhieu van tu truoc se duoc len rank ngay.

TIM KHONG LEN DIEM (da sua)
- Truoc day neu TikTok gui su kien tim chi co ma so ma thieu @ten, game tinh thanh 1 nguoi khac -> diem tim khong len va tha tim
  xac nhan khong nhan. Gio game tu noi lai (hoc ma so <-> @ten tu binh luan/qua/follow) va gop diem lai cho dung nguoi.
- Tim ma TikTok KHONG gui kem ten nguoi tha thi khong the tinh diem; moi phut game se bao 1 dong "luot tim khong ro nguoi tha".
- Neu thay nguoi tha tim lien tuc bi dau ⚠ (nghi auto-click) trong bang dieu khien thi do la bo chong auto-click dang giam diem:
  doi "Chong auto-click tim" sang "Long" hoac "Tat" de thu.
